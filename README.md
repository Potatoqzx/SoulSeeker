# SoulSeeker
/This is a shooter game. Press the number keys 1-5 and 0 to switch you characters throughout the game.
/Souls:           
/0: The defualt soul that can only move. You won't really need to use him, but he pops up if you go past your cooldowns.    
/1: Selfcontrol is the name of this soul and he moves fast    
/2: Goodness is the name of this soul and he's the one who shoots       
/3: Patients is the name of this soul and he's used to parry but he has a limited amount of time and as cooldown      
/4: Kindness is the name of this soul and he heals you but you can't move    
/5: Gentleness is the name of this soul and he makes the repels souless (the enemies) but he also has a cooldown    
/Souless:    
/These are the enemies    
/Joe: Your average joe who will die in one shot    
/Dave: He's really fast like Selfcontrol    
/Sam: He's really tanky and takes three hits to die and doesn't give any points, but is a threat becuase he deals extra damage    
/Just escape after defeating all of the Souless
